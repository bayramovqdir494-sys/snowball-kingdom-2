# Snowball Kingdom — Credits

- Game design, code, maps, environment props and UI art: made by the developer.
- Character models: base meshes generated with Tencent Hunyuan3D (AI-generated), then retopologised, rigged, animated and recoloured by the developer in Blender.
- Music: procedural (Web Audio), composed in code for this game.
- Sound effects: Kenney (www.kenney.nl) — "Interface Sounds", "Impact Sounds" packs — CC0 1.0 Public Domain. Additional effects synthesized in code.
- Font: Lilita One by Juan Montoreano — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (assets/OFL_LilitaOne.txt).
- Libraries: three.js (MIT), cannon-es (MIT), Draco decoder (Apache 2.0).

## Snowball Kingdom 2 additions
- Sled and snowmobile 3D models: generated with Tencent Hunyuan3D-2.1 (shape generation), then decimated, vertex-coloured and exported in Blender 4.2.
- Snowball Kingdom 2 logo: made from the original Snowball Kingdom logo plus a "2" set in Lilita One (SIL OFL).
- All other art and audio are reused from Snowball Kingdom (see above).

## SK2 v2 (arena rework)
